Research Overview & Key Findings

  • Reviewed reports from CBC, Maclean's, and Canadian digital divide studies detailing device access gaps in Winnipeg.
  • Sites:
    • CBC News: Winnipeg Education Digital Divide Maclean's: COVID-19 Reopening Schools CBC News: At-Home Learning Shortfall in Manitoba
  • Identified that corporate hardware retirement cycles create an abundance of functional 2012–2018 PCs.
  • Established open-source software deployment as the core strategy to bypass Windows hardware requirements and software licensing costs.
  • Defined multi-pass drive sanitization protocols and drive-less hardware acceptance options to resolve corporate privacy concerns.
