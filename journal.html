Project Journal & Research Log

Keeping track of what I learn, what I work on, and what I plan to do as the project develops.

Completed work is documented as it happens. Future entries are listed as goals and may change as the project develops.

What I Worked On

  • Looked through reports and articles from CBC, Maclean's, and other Canadian sources about access to computers and the internet in Manitoba.
  • Researched how a lack of reliable access to computers and internet can affect students and their ability to keep up with schoolwork.
  • Looked into the idea of collecting older computers that are no longer being used but could still be useful after being cleaned, repaired, or upgraded.
  • Started researching lightweight open-source operating systems that could help older computers stay useful without requiring expensive software.
  • Started looking into safe ways to handle data on donated computers before they are given to someone else.

Sources

CBC News: Winnipeg Education Digital Divide Maclean's: COVID-19 Reopening Schools CBC News: At-Home Learning Shortfall in Manitoba

Week 1 Completed. Outreach and although responses have been limited so far, the project has begun moving forward with actual device collection and support from individuals. The goals below may change as the project develops, depending on donations, responses from organizations, and available time.

Week 1 — Site & Outreach Completed

  • Launched the project website and organized the project documentation.
  • Started collecting a few donated devices from individuals interested in supporting the project.
  • Reached out to several Winnipeg businesses and organizations that may have unused computers or other equipment.
  • Received a response from the Electronic Recycling Association and spoke with them about the project.
  • Got advice from a few people about collecting equipment and starting the project.

Week 2 — Hardware Testing

  • Test open-source tools that could be used to securely wipe storage drives before donated computers are reused.
  • Test different lightweight operating systems and decide which ones work well on older hardware.
  • Work out a basic setup for the software that will be installed on refurbished computers.

Week 3 — Donations & Refurbishing

  • If donations are received, begin inspecting and testing the computers that come in.
  • Clean computers and check components such as RAM, storage, cooling, and power supplies.
  • Start keeping track of the condition and specifications of each donated computer.
  • Identify which computers can be repaired and which ones are better used for parts.

Week 4 — Preparing for Distribution

  • Work on guidelines for deciding when a refurbished computer is ready to be given to a student or family.
  • Create simple instructions to help recipients get started with the installed software.
  • Review the work completed during October and compare it with the project's longer-term goals.
  • Use what was learned during the month to plan the next stage of the project.
