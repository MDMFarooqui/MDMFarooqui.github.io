Digital Equity for Winnipeg Youth

Bridging Winnipeg's digital divide by rescuing retired hardware, deploying powerful open-source software, and putting computers directly into the hands of students in need.

Donate Hardware Our 2029 Goal

Our Mission

Access to a reliable home computer is essential for writing papers, coding, research, and general learning. Yet many students across Winnipeg still struggle without a dedicated device or rely entirely on shared smartphones.

At the same time, thousands of functional 5-to-10-year-old computers are prematurely retired by businesses every year. Digital Equity for Winnipeg Youth intercepts this hardware, refreshes it using open-source tools, and delivers it directly to local families.

Why Open Source?

Proprietary operating systems demand constant hardware upgrades and heavy system resources, forcing working computers into landfills. By deploying lightweight, community-driven open-source software, we eliminate software licensing costs, protect user privacy, and give older machines a fast, secure second life tailored for education.

Project Target

3,000

Refurbished Computers Delivered by 2029

Our long-term initiative aims to collect corporate and household e-waste across Winnipeg, install lightweight open-source operating systems, and place 3,000 fully functional PCs and laptops into student homes over the next three years.

What We Accept

We accept donations from anyone who is willing to lend a hand.

💻

Laptops & Desktops

Windows 7/8/10 era PCs, older MacBooks, or Chromebooks (roughly 2012 or newer).

🔌

Parts & Peripherals

Monitors, keyboards, mice, power adapters, spare RAM, and SSDs/HDDs.

🛠️

Faulty devices

Machines missing or having damaged components are completely welcome—we source spare parts to rebuild them.

Data Security & Privacy

Data security is essential for corporate and individual donors alike. We follow clear sanitization protocols to protect donor privacy.

Multi-Pass Sanitization

All incoming drives undergo a multi-pass overwrite process using open-source drive-wiping utilities (such as shred and DBAN) following methods similar to NIST data sanitization guidelines to ensure all previous data is irrecoverable.

Drive-Removal Option

If your organization's compliance policy requires you to destroy or retain hard drives internally, we accept machines without storage drives and will install replacement drives ourselves.

September 2026 Research & Planning

Phase 1: Mapping Winnipeg's Digital Divide

Spent the month reviewing research and journalism on digital inequity across Manitoba, including reports from CBC, Maclean's, and Canadian digital divide policy studies.

Key Takeaways: While thousands of Winnipeg students lack dedicated home PCs for schoolwork, local offices decommission functional 5-to-10-year-old computers simply because they can no longer run newer proprietary operating systems. Open-source software provides the ideal solution—turning corporate "e-waste" into reliable educational tools without licensing costs.
