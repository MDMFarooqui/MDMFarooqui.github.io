Student-Led Initiative

Digital Equity for Winnipeg Youth

Helping Winnipeg students get access to computers by collecting unused hardware, giving it a second life, and getting it into the hands of students who need it.

Donate Hardware Our 2029 Goal

Our Mission

Having a computer at home can make a big difference for students. It can be used for writing assignments, doing research, learning to code, and keeping up with schoolwork. However, not every student has access to a computer at home and some have to rely on a phone or share a device with other family members.

At the same time, many businesses and households have older computers that they no longer use. A lot of these computers can still be useful. This project is about collecting those devices, fixing them when possible, and giving them to students and families who could use them.

Why Open Source?

Older computers often struggle with newer, resource-heavy software. Lightweight open-source operating systems can help these machines run better without the need for expensive software licenses. They can also give older computers several more years of useful life instead of having them become e-waste.

Project Target

3,000

Refurbished Computers Delivered by 2029

The long-term goal is to collect unused computers from businesses and households around Winnipeg, refurbish them, and get 3,000 working computers into the homes of students and families who need them by 2029.

What We Accept

If you have old computer equipment sitting around, we'd be happy to hear from you.

💻

Laptops & Desktops

Older Windows PCs, MacBooks, and Chromebooks, especially machines from around 2012 or newer.

🔌

Parts & Peripherals

Monitors, keyboards, mice, power adapters, RAM, SSDs, HDDs, and other computer parts.

🛠️

Faulty Devices

Broken or incomplete computers are welcome too. If the device can be repaired or used for parts, we may be able to give it a second life.

Data Security & Privacy

We understand that people may have personal or business data on computers they donate. Protecting that information is an important part of the project.

Data Wiping

Before a donated computer is given to someone else, its storage drive will be securely wiped using appropriate data-erasure tools. We use established data-sanitization practices to reduce the chance of old files being recovered.

Drive-Removal Option

If a business needs to keep or destroy its storage drives for its own security or compliance requirements, that's completely fine. We can also accept computers without their original drives and install replacement storage.
