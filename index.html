Digital Equity for Winnipeg Youth

Bridging the digital divide in Winnipeg by rescuing retired hardware, installing lightweight Linux Mint, and placing computers directly into the hands of students in need.

Our Mission

Access to a reliable home computer is essential for writing papers, doing research, and succeeding in school. Yet many students across Winnipeg still struggle with device access or rely solely on shared smartphones.

At the same time, thousands of functional 5-to-10-year-old laptops and desktops are discarded or sit unused in office storage rooms. Digital Equity for Winnipeg Youth bridges this gap by intercepting retired hardware before it reaches the landfill, refurbishing it with open-source software, and delivering it to local youth.

Why Linux Mint?

Modern Windows operating systems demand heavy system specs, forcing perfectly good 2012–2018 computers into retirement. By installing lightweight, user-friendly Linux Mint, we give older hardware a second life—providing a fast, secure, and complete desktop experience for schoolwork.

What We Accept

We accept hardware from households, local small businesses, law firms, and IT providers across Winnipeg.

💻

Laptops & Desktops

Windows 7/8/10 era machines, older MacBooks, or Chromeboxes (roughly 2012 or newer).

🔌

Parts & Accessories

Power adapters, chargers, monitors, mice, keyboards, and spare RAM or SSDs.

🛠️

Fixer-Uppers

Machines with missing storage drives, weak batteries, or low RAM are completely welcome!

Data Security & Privacy Guarantee

Data security is the #1 concern for corporate and individual donors. We maintain strict sanitization standards to ensure complete peace of mind.

🔒 NIST Multi-Pass Drive Wiping

Every donated hard drive undergoes a certified multi-pass wipe using open-source drive sanitization tools (like shred and DBAN) to permanently erase all personal or corporate data before any OS is deployed.

🛠️ Drive-Removal Option

If your organization's compliance policy requires internal drive destruction or retention, we accept hardware without storage drives and will supply replacement SSDs ourselves.

Donate or Get in Touch

Have unused office computers or a household laptop gathering dust? We arrange free pickup anywhere in Winnipeg or public meetups at local library branches.
